About FindBack

A school-wide lost & found platform built for our community.

FindBack is the official digital lost & found for our school. Every year, hundreds of items are lost across campus — textbooks, laptops, ID cards, keys and more. FindBack makes it easy for students and staff to report items, search listings, and recover what belongs to them, while administrators verify every listing and claim for safety and security.

Verified Listings

Every report is reviewed by the lost & found office before it goes public, keeping the platform safe and trustworthy.

Privacy First

Contact details are kept private and only shared with administrators and the verified owner of an item.

Smart Matching

Our matching engine compares lost and found reports across campus and flags possible matches automatically.

Recovery, Tracked

From report to return, every item's journey is tracked so you always know what's happening with your things.

Our Mission

No lost item should stay lost. We bring students, staff and the school office together to return every item to its owner.
